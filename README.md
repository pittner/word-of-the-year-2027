# Word of the Year 2027

48 word of the year ideas for 2027 in 6 groups (calm and rest, growth and courage, focus and discipline, health and energy, money and work, people and joy). Each word has what it means in a year and a first step small enough for this week, plus a short guide to choosing your own word.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/word-of-the-year-2027/

- Press a word and it opens in the [free vision board maker](https://visionboard.bemooore.com/free-vision-board-maker/?mode=wallpaper&layout=text&utm_source=github_readme&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=word_2027) as a 1080x1920 phone lock screen with the first step under it
- Or type your own word and first step
- No signup, nothing uploaded, MIT licensed

Rebuild: `python3 build.py` (no dependencies)
