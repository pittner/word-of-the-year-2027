How to choose your word in 10 minutes

  1. Look back at 2026. Write the three moments you are proud of and the three you would do differently. Your word usually sits between the two lists.
  2. Ask one question. "What do I want to feel more of in 2027?" Answer with one word, not a goal. Calm, brave, strong, free.
  3. Test it with a decision. Imagine a normal busy Tuesday. Does the word tell you what to say yes or no to? If it does, keep it.
  4. Put it where you will see it. A word you see 80 times a day on your lock screen works better than one written once in a notebook.

Questions

What is a word of the year?

One word you choose to guide the coming year instead of, or next to, a list of resolutions. It names how you want to live or feel, for example calm, brave or focus, and you use it to decide what to say yes or no to.

How do I choose my word for 2027?

Look back at 2026, ask what you want to feel more of in 2027, and test the word with an ordinary decision. If it tells you what to do on a busy day, it is your word. There are 48 ideas with a meaning and a first step on this page.

What are good words of the year?

Words that point to a feeling or a way of acting: calm, brave, focus, strong, joy, finish, simplify, connect. A good word is short, positive and easy to remember when you are tired.
